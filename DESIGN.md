# Design

One landing page. Product config dyes it. Five domains, one room.

## World

A product room: the real iPhone sits in the product’s own light. Tokens
own whole regions (hero sky, blush founder band, ink privacy). Atmosphere
is layered orbs, not furniture and not a card grid.

Light or dark comes from the product. Do not invert a light product.

## First viewport

Full-height field. Headline and CTA on the left. The hero screenshot
overlaps a second real screen. If someone left after one screen they
should remember the phone in that color of light.

## Tokens

`--paper`, `--field`, `--ink`, `--accent`, lantern colors, and
`--ink-on-accent` from `site.tokens`.

Display: SF Pro Rounded. Body: SF Pro Text. Tracking never tighter than
`-0.04em`. Display stays under 6rem.

## Motion

The phone is a lantern. One sheen, then a slow float. Orbs breathe.
Gallery phones lift on hover. Reduced motion removes loops.

## Anti-references

- A five-app storefront
- Sectioned SaaS template chrome
- Unofficial App Store badges
- Mocked screens
- Client JavaScript on the landing
